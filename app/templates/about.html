About Antick Bhattacharjee

Technology educator, Python developer, and corporate trainer committed to practical, project-based engineering.

Background & Pedagogy

I work at the intersection of technical training, software engineering, and workflow automation. Throughout my work with learners, developers, and corporate teams, my core guiding principle has remained simple: Learn by building. Build with purpose.

Rather than memorizing abstract concepts or chasing fleeting toolchains, I encourage understanding computing systems from first principles. By deconstructing problems, designing architectures, and writing working code, learners develop the intuition required to solve real-world problems independently.

Technical Focus Areas

  • Python & Modern Programming: Teaching and developing backend services, web applications, and algorithmic problem-solving.
  • Corporate Training & Workshops: Delivering structured curriculum designed to upskill teams in modern tooling and engineering practices.
  • Workflow & System Automation: Creating robust scripts, API integrations, and Google Workspace automated tooling.
  • AI Experimentation: Exploring modern language models, API integrations, and AI-assisted workflows to accelerate development cycles.

Teaching Philosophy

True technical confidence comes from demystifying the black boxes. In every workshop and training session, we emphasize live debugging, incremental architecture, and writing clean, readable, and maintainable software.
